Privacy Policy

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy
Side Quest Lew Studio is a private tool that the owner of the Side Quest Lew channels runs on their own computer to edit their own gameplay recordings and post them to their own accounts. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

1. Whose data

The app only handles data belonging to its owner. It does not collect data about viewers or other people, and it does not use cookies, ads or analytics trackers.

2. What the app accesses on TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your account ID, display name and avatar, so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): to send videos the owner approved to their TikTok drafts or profile, with the privacy and interaction settings the owner chooses for each post.
  • Video list (video.list): views, likes, comments and shares on the owner's own videos, for the app's analytics page.

The app accesses the equivalent information on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook through their official APIs, and only for the owner's own accounts.

3. Where data is stored

Everything is stored on the owner's own computer and the owner's own external drive. Access tokens are kept in a local file readable only by the owner's user account. There is no app server, and the data is never sold or shared.

4. Services that process data

  • The platforms above, to receive the videos the owner posts.
  • ElevenLabs, to turn voiceover lines written for a video into speech.
  • Anthropic (Claude), when enabled, to suggest edits from still frames and text about the owner's recordings.
  • YouTube Data API, to read public trend information about gaming videos.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Recordings and edits are deleted from the owner's computer once their videos are posted, or earlier if the owner chooses. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored access token. You can also remove the app's access to TikTok at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data about anyone under 13.

7. Contact

Questions about these policies can be sent as a direct message to @sidequestlew on Instagram.

8. Changes
